Research Profile Research Papers

Since 19994 when I joined the Coastal Modelling Group in the Department of Engineering, University of Liverpool led by Prof B A O'Connor, I have been involved in a number of research projects funded by the UK EPSRC and EU, studying coastal hydrodynamics and morphodynamics with use of large scale laboratory facilities and advanced process-based numerical models. Since 1999, I have been Co-Investigator and Principal Investigator for 7 EPSRC-funded research projects. Presently I am leading a £1.1m joint research project in collaboration with Universities of East Anglia and Plymouth, as well as Proudman Oceanographic Laboratory.

EPSRC funded projects

  • LUCI/LUCIO: To Study the  wave-current interaction and turbulence over complex bathymetry (LUCI) and in the presence of river outflow and tidal currents (LUCIO)
  • LUPY: To study the hydrodynamics and morphodynamics associated with a group of nearshore breakwater (1:28 model of Elmer Breakwaters) using the large-scale Coastal Research Facility (UK CRF) at HR Wallingford
  • HYDRO: To develop a phase-resolving computer model to study the effects of wave reflection and flow leakage on current circulation with nearshore structures
  • LUBA: To study sediment entrainment over rippled bed and under sheet flow conditions using an oscillatory flow tunnel and numerical models
  • LEACOAST: To study the effects of shore-parallel breakwaters on coastal morphology in tidal and storm conditions
  • STANTOSS: To use the modelling system developed in LUBA project to the project to assist with the parameterization of the sediment transport under the sheet flow conditions for practical engineering models
  • LEACOAST2: To study the longer-term effects of shore parallel breakwaters on beaches and coasts

EU funded Projects:

  • CSTAB: To study the circulation and sediment transport around offshore sandbanks with a 3D hydrodynamic and bedform model
  • SCARCOST: To study the effects of enhanced turbulence and liquefaction on the sediment transport using laboratory tests and numerical models
  • COAST3D: To study the hydrodynamics and morphodynamics for coastal beaches in the Netherlands and the Teignmouth Estuary in the UK
  • INDIA: To study the long-term evolution and migration of a tidal inlet at Algarve, Portugal, using remote-sensing techniques and computer models
