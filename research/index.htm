My Research

Since 1994, I have been involved in research on physical and numerical modelling of coastal hydrodynamics and morphodynamcis, through a number of EU and UK research projects. Currently, much of my research is focused on the modelling the impacts of coastal structures on beaches, in particular, under tidal conditions within the EPSRC-funded LEACOAST2 project, as well as wave modelling with POLCOMS and ProWAM as part of NERC-funded EPIRUS project.
